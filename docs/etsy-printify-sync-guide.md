# KrowN Supply Co. — Etsy-to-Printify Live Synchronization & Operations Guide

## Executive Overview
This operational guide outlines the exact, step-by-step procedures to link the official **KrowN Supply Co.** Etsy storefront directly into Printify's Print-on-Demand (POD) fulfillment infrastructure for automated order routing and custom personalization handling.

---

## 1. Connecting Printify’s Etsy Sales Channel

1. **Prerequisites**:
   - Registered Etsy Seller Account with active payment deposit account.
   - Printify Merchant Account with a credit balance or linked commercial card for base production costs.
2. **Channel Linking**:
   - Log into the [Printify Dashboard](https://printify.com).
   - In the top navigation dropdown (active store menu), select **"Add a new store"**.
   - Choose **Etsy** as the sales channel.
   - Click **"Connect to Etsy"**. Printify will redirect to Etsy's OAuth permission screen.
   - Click **"Grant Access"** to authorize Printify to read orders, update shipping tracking numbers, and sync inventory levels.
3. **Store Branding**:
   - Store Name: **KrowN Supply Co.**
   - Slogan: *"WEAR THE KROWN."*

## 2. Order Approval Settings (Launch Phase vs. Scaled Autopilot)

Printify provides granular control over how orders transition from Etsy into production:

### Phase A: Initial Launch Phase (First 48 Hours) — **Manual Approval**
> **Setting**: `Orders` > `Order Approval Settings` > **"Manual"**
- **Objective**: Ensure 100% human verification of customer personalization entries, sizing selections, and shipping addresses.
- **Workflow**:
  1. An Etsy order comes in for an **AXA Pro League Custom Jersey** or **Richardson 112 Leather Patch Hat**.
  2. Order appears in Printify's **"Orders"** tab with status `On Hold / Needs Review`.
  3. Operations Architect verifies:
     - Customer Gamertag conforms to font guidelines (all-caps, ≤16 chars, no profanity).
     - Player Number is formatted between `00`–`99`.
     - Print positioning matches production proofs.
  4. Click **"Send to Production"**.

### Phase B: Scaled Autopilot Mode — **Automatic with 1-Hour Delay**
> **Setting**: `Orders` > `Order Approval Settings` > **"Automatically after 1 hour"**
- **Objective**: Zero-touch fulfillment while providing a safe 60-minute window for buyers to request address updates or typo corrections before automated manufacturing lock-in.
- **Auto-Sync Schedule**:
  - Printify queries Etsy every 15 minutes.
  - Tracking numbers generated by Monster Digital / SwiftPOD / Headwear specialists are pushed back to Etsy automatically with USPS/UPS tracking links, triggering Etsy's customer shipping notification and marking the order `Complete`.

## 3. Routing Custom Gamertag Personalization from Etsy to Printify

When an Etsy buyer places an order with personalization enabled:

### Etsy Personalization Configuration
In the Etsy listing settings (enabled on `AXA Pro League Custom Jersey`):
- **Personalization**: `Enabled (Required)`
- **Prompt to Buyer**:
  > *"Enter your custom Gamertag / Player Name (max 16 characters) and optional Squad Number (0–99). Double check spelling before purchase."*

### Automated Data Pipeline into Printify
1. Etsy includes buyer-submitted text in the `buyer_note` / `personalization` field of the order payload.
2. In Printify:
   - For standard SKUs, Printify matches the item to the configured blueprint variant.
   - For custom items, Printify populates the `metadata.custom_gamertag` and `metadata.custom_player_number` fields.
3. If an order uses manual proofing:
   - The team uses the custom graphic template in Photoshop/Illustrator, types the customer's exact Gamertag, exports the print layer, and attaches it in Printify before approving.
4. If utilizing Printify's automated text-replacement API:
   - The Printify order creation payload transmits the text directly into the designated text design layer ID.

## 5. Shipping Profiles & Policies
- **Domestic US**: Free Shipping ($0.00) baked into high-margin pricing architecture.
- **Carrier**: USPS Ground Advantage (2–5 transit days).
- **Processing Time on Etsy**: Set to `3–5 business days` to prevent late shipment strikes.
