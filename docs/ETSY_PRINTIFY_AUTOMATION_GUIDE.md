## 3. Step-by-Step Printify-to-Etsy Connection

### Step 1: Open Your Etsy Shop (If not already created)
1. Go to [Etsy.com](https://www.etsy.com) and click **Sell on Etsy**.
2. Create your shop name (e.g., `KrowNSupplyCo` or `KrowNGoods`).
3. Set your currency to **USD** and complete billing info.

### Step 2: Link Printify to Etsy
1. Log in to your [Printify Dashboard](https://printify.com).
2. Click the store dropdown in the top-right and select **"Add a new store"**.
3. Choose **Etsy**.
4. Click **"Connect to Etsy"** — this will open an Etsy authorization window.
5. Click **"Grant Access"**.

### Step 3: Enable 100% Automated Order Routing
In your Printify settings:
1. Navigate to **Store Settings → Order Approval**.
2. Select **"Automatically (in 1 hour)"** or **"Automatically (in 24 hours)"**.
   * *Why a 1-hour delay?* This gives buyers 1 hour to change their shipping address or cancel before the print provider charges you and starts printing.
3. Turn on **"Order Tracking Notification"** so Etsy automatically updates customers with their USPS tracking number the minute it ships.
