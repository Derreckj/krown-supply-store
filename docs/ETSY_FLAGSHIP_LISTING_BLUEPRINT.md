# KrowN Supply Co. — Etsy Flagship Listing Blueprint & Scale Strategy

## Executive Summary
This blueprint operationalizes the **Dual-Engine Acquisition Model** for **KrowN Supply Co.** on Etsy, derived from competitive analysis of top 10 Etsy apparel/hat powerhouses (`happystackshop` with 34.9K sales, `83andPineLLC` with 28.5K sales, `StunningHats` with 57.4K sales, and `EmblifeDesign` with 59.2K sales).

Rather than uploading an unfocused 100-item POD catalog, KrowN operates on a **high-ticket, high-search-intent Flagship Anchor**:
1. **Engine 1 (Brand Retail Drop):** Authentic Richardson 112 Leather Patch Hats & 3D Puff Embroidery Truckers ($29.99 launch / $34.99 reg).
2. **Engine 2 (B2B / Custom Volume Engine):** "Custom KrowN Works" Custom Richardson 112 Leather Patch Hats for construction crews, contractors, trades, and esports teams with tiered quantity pricing (1, 6, 12, 24 hats).

## 3. The 10-Slide Etsy Photo Stack Formula

High-converting Etsy hat shops achieve 3-5% conversion rates by following this strict visual sequence:

| Slide # | Visual Asset | Graphic / Overlay Copy | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **01** | **Main Hero Thumbnail** | Clean 45° angle shot on granite boulder or rustic workbench, natural daylight, zero cluttered badges. | Highest possible CTR in Etsy search results. Must fill 75-80% of canvas. |
| **02** | **Colorway Swatch Matrix** | Grid showing Richardson 112 color combinations (Khaki/Coffee, Solid Black, Heather/Black, Navy/White). | Answers "what colors are available?" in 2 seconds. |
| **03** | **Leather & Stitch Macro Close-up** | Extreme close-up of 100% genuine vegetable-tanned leather grain and perimeter saddle stitching. | Proves premium craftsmanship (not cheap glued faux leather). |
| **04** | **Authenticity Callout** | Richardson 112 visor foil sticker and interior woven label close-up. | Establishes genuine Richardson brand authority. |
| **05** | **Profile & Fit Guide** | Side profile showing structured mid-pro crown, pre-curved visor, and adjustable 7-snap closure. | Eliminates sizing anxiety for buyers. |
| **06** | **3-Step Order Process** | 1. Choose Hat & Patch Color → 2. Upload Logo / Text → 3. Approve 24h Digital Proof. | Removes confusion for custom and gift buyers. |
| **07** | **Volume Tier Pricing Table** | Crew 6-Pack (10% off), Company 12-Pack (15% off), Fleet 24-Pack (20% off). | Drives high-AOV commercial B2B orders ($162 - $576 carts). |
| **08** | **Lifestyle / On-Model Shot** | Tradesman on jobsite or model wearing hat outdoors. | Emotional connection and scale reference. |
| **09** | **5-Star Customer Review Showcase** | Real screenshot mockup of 5-star customer review praising leather quality and fast shipping. | Social proof trigger. |
| **10** | **Packaging & Shipping Guarantee** | Hat shipped in rigid cardboard box (never a poly mailer bag) with protective tissue. | Relieves fear of crushed crowns during transit. |
